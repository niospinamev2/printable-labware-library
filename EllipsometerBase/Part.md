# Part Name

## Description

The ellipsometer base is designed to be mounted on the sample stage of an ellipsometer. Its purpose is to ensure repeatable wafer positioning across multiple characterization runs.

The assembly consists of six parts, each serving a specific structural or functional purpose.

### Components

**001 — Core Plate**

The core plate is the main structural component of the assembly. It supports the wafer and provides the foundation for the remaining components.

**002–004 — Main Ribs**

These ribs are arranged around the core plate and conform to the cylindrical shape of the sample stage. They form the main surrounding structure of the base, allowing it to fit around the stage.

**005–006 — Reinforcement Ribs**

The reinforcement ribs are mounted around the main ribs to increase their structural rigidity. They also provide support for the heat-set inserts, which accommodate screws used to secure the assembly to the sample stage.
